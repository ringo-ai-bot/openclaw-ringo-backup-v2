```text

Total lender return   = Rp3,500,000 + Rp525,000 = Rp4,025,000
Monthly return        = Rp4,025,000 ÷ 6
                      = Rp670,833.33
```

So the six scheduled lender returns would be approximately:

- Periods 1–5: *Rp670,833.33*
- Final period: *Rp670,833.35*

user: how to compute monthly lender rate for annual 15% and tenor 6 months
assistant: For a *15% annual flat rate* with a *6-month tenor*, use:

```text
Monthly flat rate = Annual flat rate ÷ tenor
                  = 15% ÷ 6
                  = 2.5% of original principal per month
```

For Rp3,500,000:

```text
Monthly interest = Rp3,500,000 × 2.5%
                 = Rp87,500

Monthly principal = Rp3,500,000 ÷ 6
                  = Rp583,333.33

Monthly lender return = Rp583,333.33 + Rp87,500
                      = Rp670,833.33
```

So: *2.5% monthly* is the rate equivalent when the full 15% is charged across a 6-month flat-tenor loan.

Don’t use `15% ÷ 12 = 1.25%` if the business rule is “15% for the entire loan tenor.” That would only produce Rp262,500 total interest over six months.
